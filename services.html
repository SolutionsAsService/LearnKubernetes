Services

Pods are replaced and get new addresses. A Service gives them one name and one address that does not change.

Which pods does the Service reach?

The Service selects pods by label. Activate a pod to flip its label between app=web and app=batch.

Service web-svc selector: app=web
Turn on JavaScript to flip labels. Pods labelled app=web are the Service's endpoints.

Three ways to expose pods

  • ClusterIP (the default): reachable only inside the cluster. Use it for service-to-service traffic.
  • NodePort: opens the same port on every node, in the range 30000 to 32767 by default.
  • LoadBalancer: asks your cloud provider for an external load balancer. On a laptop cluster it needs extra help to get an address.

For HTTP routing by host name and path, an Ingress sits in front of Services, and needs an ingress controller installed in the cluster.

Common mistake. Expecting a pod's own IP to stay put. It will not. Talk to the Service, never to a pod address.
